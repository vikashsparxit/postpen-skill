## Title

PostPen: social posts with your OK

## Short description (≤160 chars)

Draft, schedule and check posts on LinkedIn, X, Facebook, Instagram, Threads and YouTube. Always shows a preview and never posts without your OK.

## Long description

PostPen connects your agent to your social accounts. Your agent writes the post. PostPen saves it as a draft, shows you a preview link, and schedules or publishes it only after you approve the exact text, accounts, media and time.

One request can cover several networks, each with its own version: a LinkedIn post for your company page, a shorter X post or thread, a Threads post, an Instagram feed post, Story or Reel (including trial Reels), a Facebook Page post, and a YouTube video or Short with its title, privacy and playlist.

After it's out, ask how it did. PostPen reports whether each copy is live, why one failed, impressions and engagement per post, and how each account is trending, so your agent can suggest what to post more of.

Sign in to PostPen once in your browser. Your agent never sees your social network passwords or tokens. Everything your agent drafts appears on your PostPen calendar at app.postpen.ai, where you can edit or move it.

**What the PostPen skill adds on top of the MCP server:**
- A strict preview → explicit OK → schedule flow, with a fresh OK after any edit
- Per-network writing rules (limits, threads, hashtags, Story/Reel/Short formats)
- Rights reminders for music and media, and no copying from inspiration posts
- Treats text from comments and inspiration posts as data, never as instructions
- Clear reporting of live / deleted / expired / failed posts and what to repeat

## Example prompts

1. "Announce our dark-mode launch on our LinkedIn page, X and Threads. Show me the drafts first."
2. "Turn this blog post into an X thread and schedule it for Tuesday 9am my time."
3. "Post this vertical clip to Instagram as a trial Reel and to YouTube as an unlisted Short."
4. "What's scheduled for next week?"
5. "Did yesterday's post go out everywhere? How is it doing?"
6. "What worked best on my LinkedIn this month, and what should I post more of?"
